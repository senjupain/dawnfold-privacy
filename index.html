Dawnfold Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Dawnfold is built by Akatsuki1 on a simple principle: your documents are yours. This policy is short because there isn't much to say — Dawnfold doesn't have a server, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.

What Dawnfold does

Dawnfold scans documents using your device's camera, recognizes text using on-device text recognition, and generates a PDF — all locally on your phone.

What Dawnfold collects

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in. Dawnfold doesn't ask who you are.
  • No cloud storage. Every scan, every PDF, and every piece of recognized text is stored only in Dawnfold's local app storage on your device.
  • No analytics or tracking. Dawnfold doesn't monitor how you use the app.
  • No ads, no ad networks. Nothing in Dawnfold talks to an advertiser.
  • No network requests at all, from the app itself, related to your documents or usage.

Permissions Dawnfold requests

  • Camera — required to scan documents. Used only while you're actively scanning; nothing is transmitted anywhere.

Data deletion

Since nothing leaves your device, deleting a document inside Dawnfold (or uninstalling the app) permanently removes that data — there's no remote copy to separately delete.

Changes to this policy

If Dawnfold's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the update will be reflected in the app's store listing before the change takes effect.
